Frequently asked questions

What does Potluck Labs, Inc. do?

Yes. Contact us for a scoped proposal for software development, ghostwriting, manuscript preparation, and related professional work. Pricing and deliverables are agreed in writing before payment.

Potluck Labs contributes to PocketAuthor development. The legal merchant for any PocketAuthor purchase must be identified in the PocketAuthor checkout and terms; this website does not claim to sell PocketAuthor plans or credits.

No. This is our corporate information website, not a checkout or storefront. Paid products, if offered, will identify what is sold and provide purchase terms before payment.

Our products are digital software. Each released paid product will state its delivery and access method at purchase. This site does not sell or ship physical merchandise.

There is no transaction currency on this corporate site because purchases are not offered here. Any paid product must display its actual currency, price, and billing period before checkout.

Email contact@potluck.software with the product name and relevant purchase details. Do not email full payment card information.

No. The business activities described here are consumer software development and product research. We do not offer regulated financial services through this corporate website.
